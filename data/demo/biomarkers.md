# Biomarker-guided therapy in the BR-01 research cohort

The BR-01 observational cohort investigated whether the protein biomarker B7-H3
was associated with response to an experimental immune therapy. Participants
with high baseline B7-H3 expression had a higher observed response rate than
participants with low expression. This association is exploratory and does not
establish that B7-H3 causes treatment response.

The analysis adjusted for age and disease stage, but the cohort was small and
not randomized. The authors recommend validation in a larger prospective
cohort before using the biomarker for treatment selection. Adverse events and
overall survival were not assessed in this demonstration dataset.
